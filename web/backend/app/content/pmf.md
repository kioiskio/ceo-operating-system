# Product-Market Fit Framework

> **Stage:** Pre-seed through Series A  
> **Last updated:** May 2026

PMF as a spectrum you continuously improve — not a binary switch.

## The PMF Spectrum

```
No PMF ──── Weak PMF ──── Strong PMF ──── Extreme PMF
```

---

## How to Measure PMF

### Leading Indicators (early-stage)

| Metric | Weak PMF | Strong PMF |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Retention (D30) | <20% | >40% consumer / >70% B2B SaaS |
| Organic growth | <10% new users | >30% |
| Time to value | >7 days | <1 day B2B |
| Customer calls | "Interesting" | "When can I pay?" |
| NPS | <20 | >40 |

### Lagging Indicators (growth-stage)

| Metric | Weak PMF | Strong PMF |
|--------|----------|------------|
| NRR | <100% | >120% |
| Logo churn (monthly) | >5% | <2% |
| Expansion revenue | <10% | >30% of new ARR |
| Sales cycle | >90 days | <30 days |
| Win rate | <20% | >40% |

---

## The PMF Engine: 4 Levers

1. **Problem validation** — frequent, painful, urgent, recognized by customer.
2. **Solution fit** — before/after transformation in customer's words.
3. **Segment precision** — one ICP vertical before horizontal expansion.
4. **Value delivery speed** — aha <5 min consumer / <1 day B2B.

---

## Sean Ellis Test

> "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?"

**Benchmark:** >40% "very disappointed" → PMF signal; <25% → no PMF.

---

## PMF Playbook by Stage

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| Pre-seed | 50 customer interviews; design partners; don't over-build |
| Seed | Ship core transformation; D7/D30 retention; kill non-retention features |
| Series A | Double down on best segment; expansion revenue; sharp ICP |

---

## Warning Signs You Don't Have PMF

Explaining the product yourself; growth only via spend; flat/worsening churn; roadmap driven by prospects; lukewarm interviews; "we need more features" as default answer.
