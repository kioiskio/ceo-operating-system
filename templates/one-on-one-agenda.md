# 1-on-1 Meeting Agenda

> A repeatable template for effective founder-to-report conversations.
> Designed for 30-minute weekly cadence. Adjust for biweekly or 45-minute slots.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. **Direct report fills out** the agenda 24 hours before the meeting
2. **You (the manager/CEO) review** before walking in
3. **Stick to the structure.** Don't let it become a status update — that's what async tools are for

## Rules for the Manager (Read Before Every 1-on-1)

1. **This is their time, not yours.** You should talk <30% of the time.
2. **Don't cancel.** Reschedule if necessary, but never skip. Canceling signals they're not a priority.
3. **No status updates.** If you're asking "what's the status on X?" you've failed. Use async tools for that.
4. **Take notes.** Write down action items and follow up next week. Nothing erodes trust faster than forgetting.
5. **Ask, don't tell.** When they're stuck, ask "What have you tried?" before offering solutions.
6. **Feedback is a gift.** If you have nothing to say in the feedback section, you're not paying attention.
7. **Personal check-in comes first.** If they're going through something, that's the whole meeting. The work can wait.

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Wins section is consistently empty | They're stuck or disengaged — dig in |
| "I'm fine" every week | They're not fine, they're just not telling you |
| No blockers ever listed | Either everything is perfect (unlikely) or they don't trust you with problems |
| Action items never get done | Priorities are unclear or workload is unsustainable |
| They stop filling out the agenda | They've given up on the format — ask why |

---

## Scaling: From 5 to 50 Reports

- **<10 reports:** Weekly, 30 min each. You can do this.
- **10-25 reports:** Biweekly, 30 min. Supplement with a weekly async check-in (Slack/email).
- **25-50 reports:** Monthly 1-on-1s with direct reports. Skip-level 1-on-1s quarterly with their reports.
- **50+:** You should not be doing 1-on-1s with everyone. Focus on your direct reports and key ICs.
